# 12-Week Athlete Build

A five-day training plan for speed, strength, mobility, back health and conditioning, with a built-in log. It runs in your phone's browser, saves your log on the device, and works offline after the first visit. No account or app needed.

**Open it:** https://vedantnissar.github.io/athlete-build/

## Use it on your phone

1. Open the link above in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
2. Add it to your home screen. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android: ⋮ → Add to home screen (or Install app).
3. Always open it the same way. On iPhone, the home-screen icon and Safari keep separate logs.
4. Back up your log every week or two: Progress tab → Copy backup, then paste the code into your Notes app.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole app, with no build step or dependencies
- `sw.js`: makes it work offline
- `manifest.webmanifest` and `icon-*.png`: the home-screen icon

The site is published with GitHub Pages from the `main` branch (Settings → Pages).

General training guidance, not medical advice. If you have pain, an injury or a medical condition, get checked first.
